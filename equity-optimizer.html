Home Equity Optimizer™

A smarter way to use home equity, designed around your cash flow rather than just your payment. Your paycheck stops sitting idle and starts lowering the interest you pay every day.

The short answer

The Fairway Home Equity Optimizer™ is a first-lien home equity line of credit (HELOC) linked to a checking account. Every night, the money in that account is swept against your loan balance, so interest is charged on a lower amount while you keep full access to your funds.

How it works

Same income. Same spending. Very different results.

You use the linked account just like everyday checking. The difference is what happens to your balance overnight.

  1. 01

    Deposit

    Your paychecks and other income go into the checking account linked to your loan.

  2. 02

    Nightly sweep

    Each night, your available balance is automatically applied against the loan. No extra payment needed.

  3. 03

    Less interest

    Interest accrues daily, so it’s calculated on the lower balance, even if the money was only there a short time.

  4. 04

    Full access

    Your debit card, checks, transfers and bill pay all still work. Spending simply draws on the line again.

Indications

Who it’s for

The Home Equity Optimizer™ works best when more money comes into your account each month than goes out. The bigger that monthly surplus, the faster your balance falls.

A good fit if you

  • Have consistent income and usually end the month with money left over
  • Manage your cash flow thoughtfully and like seeing where it goes
  • Value flexibility: access to your equity whenever you need it, with no prepayment penalties
  • Own your home free and clear and want access to equity without a traditional mortgage

Worth a careful look if you

  • Prefer a fixed rate and a payment that never changes. This loan has a variable rate
  • Often spend as much as you earn. Without a monthly surplus, payoff won’t speed up much

Questions

Home Equity Optimizer™ FAQs

What is the Fairway Home Equity Optimizer™?

It’s a first-lien home equity line of credit (HELOC) designed to help you build equity faster, reduce interest costs and keep flexible access to funds. It comes with a linked checking account that sweeps deposits nightly against your balance, a credit limit that stays fixed for the first 10 years and then reduces by 1/240th per month over the remaining 20, and flexible payment options including interest-only, traditional amortization, and no payment as the credit limit allows.

What does “sweep” mean?

A sweep is an automatic process that applies the available balance in your linked checking account to your loan balance overnight. It lowers the balance used to calculate interest without you making an extra payment or giving up access to your money.

Does interest really accrue daily?

Yes. Interest accrues daily on your outstanding balance, so deposits start reducing interest right away, even if the money is only in the account for a short time.

Can I still access my money after it sweeps?

Yes. You can still use available funds, make transfers, write checks and use your debit card. The sweep changes how interest is calculated; it doesn’t lock your money away. Using funds after a sweep increases your outstanding balance again, like using a line of credit.

Do I have to deposit my paycheck into this account?

No, it isn’t required. But regular deposits are what drive the interest savings, so depositing your income there gets you the most out of the program.

How are my mortgage payments made?

You don’t send a payment to Fairway or the servicer. Once the account is set up, payments are swept automatically from the linked checking account on the 21st of each month.

How quickly can I pay off my loan?

It depends on your deposits and spending. Borrowers who consistently deposit more than they spend may be able to pay off significantly faster and pay less total interest, without changing their lifestyle or refinancing. Try the calculator above with your own numbers.

Is this a fixed or variable rate loan?

Variable. The rate is a market index plus a fixed margin and can change over time with market conditions. Your loan documents explain exactly how it’s set and adjusted.

Is my money safe in the checking account?

The checking account is held with an FDIC-insured bank and works like standard checking, with a debit card, check writing, bill pay and online banking. Your funds stay accessible, subject to available credit and account terms.

Can this loan be used on a home I own free and clear?

Yes, as long as the minimum draw requirement is met. That lets you access your equity without taking out a traditional mortgage.

02 — The Consultation

How a home loan gets filled.

The same order of operations a good pharmacist follows: understand the patient, check the details, dispense with clear directions, and follow up.

  1. i.

    Intake

    A no-pressure conversation about your goals, budget, timeline, and anything that worries you — credit, savings, or the rate headlines.

  2. ii.

    Review

    Diane reviews income, credit, and assets, then tells you exactly what she needs from you, in a simple checklist. No guesswork.

  3. iii.

    Prescribe

    You get a side-by-side of the programs you qualify for, with payments and cash-to-close explained, and a pre-approval you can shop with.

  4. iv.

    Follow-up

    Through underwriting, appraisal, and closing, Diane keeps you, your agent, and your title company informed — and stays your resource after.

04 — Common Symptoms

What keeps people from buying — and what to do about it.

  • 01

    “I don’t think I can afford it.”

    Often the real number is different than people expect. Low-down-payment programs and local assistance can change the math — Diane will show you the actual payment, not a guess.

  • 02

    “My credit isn’t good enough.”

    Some programs are built for scores still being rebuilt. If you aren’t ready yet, you’ll leave with a specific plan to get there — not a “no.”

  • 03

    “Rates are too high right now.”

    Rates matter, but so do price, timing, and your plans. Diane will walk you through buy-downs, terms, and when a future refinance could make sense.

Questions, answered plainly.

Who is Diane Pepe?

Diane Pepe is a mortgage loan officer with Fairway Independent Mortgage Corporation. She serves home buyers and homeowners in the Denver Metro area of Colorado and in Lexington, Nebraska. Before becoming a loan officer she was a pharmacist for 25 years, and she is known as The Mortgage Pharmacist.

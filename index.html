Quick Answers

Questions, answered plainly.

Who is Diane Pepe?

Diane Pepe is a mortgage loan officer with Fairway Independent Mortgage Corporation. She serves home buyers and homeowners in the Denver Metro area of Colorado and in Lexington, Nebraska. Before becoming a loan officer she was a pharmacist for 25 years, and she is known as The Mortgage Pharmacist.

Why is she called The Mortgage Pharmacist?

Diane practiced pharmacy for 25 years before moving into mortgage lending. She brings the same habits to home loans: listening carefully, reviewing every detail, explaining things in plain language, and matching each client to the loan program that fits their situation.

What areas does Diane Pepe serve?

Diane focuses on the Denver Metro area, including Douglas County communities such as Franktown, Parker and Castle Rock, and on Lexington, Nebraska and the surrounding area.

What types of home loans does Diane offer?

First-time home buyer loans, FHA loans, VA loans, conventional loans, luxury and jumbo loans, refinancing, investment property loans, new construction financing, relocation loans, and loans for people downsizing. See every program.

How do I get started?

Call or text Diane at (303) 249-0023, email diane.pepe@home.com, or request a consultation below. The first conversation is free and there is no obligation.
